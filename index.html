The smart home that happens to drive

Everything below runs on a Raspberry 5 mounted inside Stanley, on Home Assistant, talking to devices over Bluetooth, Zigbee, and Wi-Fi. No cloud dependency required to keep the lights on.

One dashboard, the whole van

Battery state of charge, propane level, cabin temperature, tire-terrain-ready status — all in one place, updated live, with history kept in Grafana so we can see trends over an entire trip.

National Park Foundation Edition

A limited package built for going further off the pavement: black alloy wheels, BFGoodrich All-Terrain tires, front & rear SumoSprings, a roll-up side screen door, and a 4-module Pure³ Advanced Energy System with portable solar — the electrical backbone the Home Assistant setup builds on top of. Part of every sale supports the National Park Foundation.
